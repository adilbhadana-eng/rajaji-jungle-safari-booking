Explore the Safari Zones of Rajaji
Every zone of Rajaji offers a different perspective of the wilderness. At Sukoon Safari, we
help you choose the experience that best matches your interests—.
🐘 Chilla Zone
One of Rajaji’s most popular zones for visitors seeking a classic wildlife safari experience. Its
diverse forest landscape offers opportunities to experience the rich wildlife and natural
beauty of Rajaji.
📷 Chilla Wali
A renowned destination for leopard photography, particularly valued by wildlife
photographers. The experience is designed for guests who want to spend time observing
and photographing leopards in their natural habitat.
🐆 Ranipur Zone Known for opportunities to encounter leopards and elephants. We recommend Ranipur as
an additional safari experience, especially after exploring the Jhilmil Jheel landscape.
🐅🦌 Jhilmil Jheel Zone
Our most recommended experience for wildlife and nature enthusiasts. This unique wetland
landscape offers opportunities to explore tigers, leopards and elephants, while its rich
wetland habitat makes it an excellent destination for birding and wildlife photography.
🌿 Motichur Zone
A peaceful forest experience offering a different side of Rajaji. Wildlife sightings can be
comparatively less frequent, so we generally recommend other zones first for guests whose
primary interest is wildlife sightings.
Choose your zone. Discover your wilderness. Experience Rajaji with Sukoon Safari.

Frequently Asked Questions
1. What does Sukoon Safari offer?
Sukoon Safari offers wildlife and nature experiences across Rajaji National Park, including
jungle safaris, tiger and leopard sightings, elephant experiences, birding, wildlife
photography, nature walks, jungle walks, nature trails and Van Gujjar tribal experiences.
2. Which zones of Rajaji National Park do you cover?
We facilitate safari and nature experiences across the permitted and operational zones of
Rajaji National Park, subject to forest department regulations and availability.
3. Can I book a safari with an experienced guide and driver?
Yes. Our experiences are conducted with experienced drivers, guides and naturalists who
have extensive knowledge of Rajaji's forests and wildlife.
4. Do you offer bird-watching and bird-photography tours?
Yes. We offer dedicated birding and bird-photography experiences for visitors interested in
exploring Rajaji's rich avian diversity.
5. Can I book a wildlife photography experience?
Yes. We can arrange wildlife and bird-photography experiences with knowledgeable local
guides and naturalists, subject to park rules and wildlife conditions.
6. Do you offer Van Gujjar tribal experiences?
Yes. We offer authentic cultural experiences that introduce visitors to the heritage, lifestyle
and traditions of the Van Gujjar community, with respect for local customs and community
consent.
7. Is wildlife sighting guaranteed?
No. Wildlife sightings are completely natural and cannot be guaranteed. Our team focuses
on providing a knowledgeable and responsible experience while respecting wildlife and its
habitat.
8. What is the best time to visit Rajaji National Park?
The ideal time depends on the experience you are looking for, such as wildlife, birding or
nature exploration. Our team can help you choose the most suitable season and experience.
9. Do I need to book my safari in advance? Advance booking is recommended, especially during peak seasons, weekends and holidays.
Availability is subject to park permits and forest department regulations.
10. How can I book a Sukoon Safari experience?
You can contact our team through WhatsApp, phone or the booking form on our website.
Share your preferred date, number of guests and experience, and our team will assist you
with the available options.
11. Do you provide customized wildlife experiences?
Yes. Depending on availability and applicable park regulations, we can help plan
experiences around wildlife, birding, photography, nature walks and cultural exploration.
12. Do you follow responsible wildlife-tourism practices?
Absolutely. We encourage responsible tourism by respecting wildlife, maintaining appropriate
distances, following park regulations and promoting awareness about nature and local
communities.

Terms & Cancellation Policy
Terms & Conditions
By booking an experience with Sukoon Safari, guests agree to follow the rules and
regulations of Rajaji National Park and the relevant Forest Department authorities.
Safari permits, vehicle allocation, entry timings, zones and other park-related arrangements
are subject to government regulations, availability and operational conditions. Wildlife
sightings are natural and therefore cannot be guaranteed.
Guests are expected to respect wildlife, maintain appropriate distances, follow the
instructions of guides and drivers, and avoid any activity that may disturb animals or damage
the environment.
Sukoon Safari reserves the right to modify, reschedule or cancel an experience when
required due to park closures, weather conditions, government restrictions, safety concerns
or other circumstances beyond our control.
Guests are responsible for providing accurate booking information and arriving at the
designated meeting point on time. Any applicable entry fees, permits or government charges
will be communicated during the booking process.
Cancellation & Refund Policy Cancellation and refund eligibility depends on the type of safari, permit and booking
arrangements made for the guest.
- Guest cancellation: Cancellation charges may apply depending on how close the
cancellation is to the scheduled safari date.
- No-show or late arrival: Generally, no refund will be provided for missed safaris due to late
arrival or failure to appear at the designated location.
- Park closure or government restriction: If a safari is cancelled because of an official park
closure, government order or other circumstances beyond our control, Sukoon Safari will
assist with rescheduling or applicable refund options, subject to the terms of the relevant
permit/operator.
- Weather: Safari operations may be affected by severe weather or safety conditions. Any
refund or rescheduling will be subject to the applicable park and permit rules.
- Wildlife sightings: Refunds will not be provided because a particular animal or bird was not
sighted.
For cancellation, refund or rescheduling requests, guests should contact Sukoon Safari as
early as possible with their booking details.
Note: Specific cancellation charges and refund terms will be communicated and confirmed at
the time of booking.
